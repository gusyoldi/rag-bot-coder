# Product discovery

Product discovery is the continuous work of reducing uncertainty about problems,
solutions, and delivery before (and while) building.

## Dual-track idea

Many teams run **discovery** and **delivery** in parallel:

- Discovery: interviews, prototypes, experiments, metric definitions.
- Delivery: building production-ready increments that passed enough validation.

The Product Owner connects both tracks: deciding what is worth building next and
what still needs evidence.

## Opportunity assessment

Before committing a big bet, clarify:

1. **Problem:** Who hurts and how often?
2. **Outcome:** What measurable change matters?
3. **Assumptions:** What must be true for this to work?
4. **Riskiest assumption:** What would kill the idea if false?
5. **Cheapest test:** Interview, fake door, concierge, prototype, A/B, etc.

## From insight to backlog

- Turn validated opportunities into outcomes and then into stories.
- Keep experiments and production work visible so stakeholders see learning, not
  only shipping.
- Kill or reshape ideas that fail tests instead of defending the original pitch.

## Anti-patterns

- Discovery theater: workshops with no decisions or tests.
- Building the roadmap as a feature list with no outcomes.
- Waiting for "perfect research" instead of small sequential bets.
- Ignoring operational constraints (support, compliance, tech debt) until launch.
